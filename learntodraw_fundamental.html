Fundamentals

Basics in Artistry

Here at Bibe-Ginner we believe having a strong foundation to hone your skills can help you improve in the long run. Once you master these fundamentals you’ll have a better understanding on how to accurately portray the subject and approach the matter with more confidence. We want you to develop your drawing skills, but this will all depend on your ability to follow instructions. Do you think you have what it takes to learn little duckling?

Where to Begin

As a member of our pond, Bibe-Ginner offers you a library of resources you can study to deepen your knowledge and understand the basics in line, structure, and perspective that can strengthen your understanding on form and how to draw anything from the ground up. You can upload your analysis and explore resources such as blogs, videos and talks. Engage with Bibe-Ginner—an evolving platform for sharing design methods and case studies. All members are welcome to contribute and benefit.
