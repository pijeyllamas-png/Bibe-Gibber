Meet the Community of Ducklings on Discord!

In Bibe-Ginner, we are a community that strives to hone our craft, one drawing a day. Find us on Discord if you have any other inquiries or if you wish to meet other ducklings who have the same taste in art like you! Join us on our collective studies where we critique each other and appreciate so we can improve at drawing while we chat! Until then, fly high!
