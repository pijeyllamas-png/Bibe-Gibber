BIBE-GINNER

Bibe-ginner aims to assist beginner artists who haven’t yet to start their creative journey! Art starts from within - and it starts with you. Bibe-ginner will provide you with the most efficient starter kit every duckling artist needs!

EXPLORE

What to expect

Here at Bibe-Ginner we offer references and resources in order to improve one’s art skills. Regardless of age, we intend to help every duckling find their footing in the art world and work towards becoming a better artist.

What you can gain

Here we encourage community amongst ducklings so that we can build each other up and improve together by sharing tips and tricks and what we’ve learned from each resource.

What we stand for

We as a community strive to improve one small step at a time. We want to encourage each other to try and draw everyday regardless of how the output looks. The path to success may be full of waves, but here at Bibe-Ginner we aim to strive regardless and uplift each other to do the best we can.
