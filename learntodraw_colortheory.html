Color Theory

From a Duck’s Point of View

Have you ever seen an egg painted just right? Or an outfit you love to wear, with just the right accessories to match? You ever wonder just how and why certain colours work together to evoke a feeling from you? In the visual arts, color theory is the body of practical guidance for color mixing and the visual effects of a specific color combination. It explains why certain combinations feel harmonious, why others create tension, and how ducklings like you can use colours to set the mood for each art piece.

Where to Begin

As a member of our pond, Bibe-Ginner offers you a library of resources you can study to deepen your knowledge and understand the basics in line, structure, and perspective that can strengthen your understanding on form and how to draw anything from the ground up. You can upload your analysis and explore resources such as blogs, videos and talks. Engage with Bibe-Ginner—an evolving platform for sharing design methods and case studies. All members are welcome to contribute and benefit.
