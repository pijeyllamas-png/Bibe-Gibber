From a Duck’s Point of View

So all this techy stuff’s too complicated and you can’t seem to find your footing in the digital world, fret not! Anyone can make art regardless of medium! Did you think Vincent Van Gogh had an ipad back in his day? Get out of here! Here we admire all kinds of art be it digital or traditional, and in your case little duckling, if it’s traditional you want then be it! There are a myriad of tools you can choose from, so let’s help you get started.
